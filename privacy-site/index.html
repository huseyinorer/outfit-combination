Your wardrobe

Your original clothing photos and wardrobe records remain in the app's private storage on your device.

Helpful recognition

With your permission, a reduced copy of a selected photo can be processed to suggest colors, style, and other clothing details.

Real outfit ideas

Suggestions reference the IDs and photos of clothes already saved in your wardrobe, with an offline rules-based option available.

Google sign-in is optional for wardrobe storage

Google sign-in keeps AI usage rights connected to your account. Outfit Combination receives a verified email address, display name, stable Google account identifier, and optional profile-picture URL. It never receives or stores your Google password. Your wardrobe and original clothing photos are not uploaded to your account.
