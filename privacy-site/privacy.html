Outfit Combination Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Outfit Combination Android application processes data.

1. Data controller and contact

The application is developed by Hüseyin Örer. You can use the support and privacy request page for privacy-related requests. The developer identity and contact method must match the app store listing before publication.

2. Data stored on your device

Clothing photos, clothing attributes, drafts, favorite outfits, feedback, suggestion history, wear history, learned corrections, theme/language choices, and app settings are stored in the app's private local storage. Each signed-in account sees a separate local wardrobe on the same phone. Switching accounts never deletes another local wardrobe.

3. Optional account data

Google sign-in provides a stable Google subject, verified email, display name, and optional picture URL. Passwordless email access processes a verified email address and short-lived one-time-code records. The backend assigns a random internal user UUID; it stores no passwords or Google access/refresh tokens. AI counters apply to both that account and the app installation to prevent limit resets through account switching.

4. Data processed when AI is enabled

After you provide explicit consent, a reduced-size clothing photo is sent to the backend hosted on Render and to the OpenAI API for clothing analysis. Outfit requests contain only your preferences, random clothing identifiers, and textual clothing attributes. They do not contain photos, local file paths, image URLs, or base64 image data.

5. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed only to authenticate an optional account, assign AI credits, identify clothing attributes, generate outfit suggestions, prevent abuse, control service costs, and maintain service security. AI processing is based on your explicit consent. Manual clothing entry and Quick Outfits remain available without consent or an account.

6. Retention

The backend does not permanently store your wardrobe or clothing photos in its database. OpenAI requests use store=false. According to OpenAI's official API data controls, API content is not used to train models by default, while abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days. Account profile data remains until you delete the account or request deletion. Pseudonymous AI usage records may remain separated from the deleted profile for abuse and cost control and are deleted when they are older than 13 months.

7. Service providers and international transfers

Google provides optional authentication, the configured email provider delivers one-time codes, Render provides backend hosting, Neon provides the account and usage database, and OpenAI provides image and text analysis. Their infrastructure may process data outside Türkiye. Provider secrets are stored only in the backend environment and are never embedded in the mobile app.

8. Deletion and your rights

You can delete clothing records, photos, favorites, drafts, histories, learned corrections, and settings from this device by selecting Bu cihazdaki tüm verileri sil in Settings. If signed in, Hesabı sil removes the Google identifier, email, display name, picture URL, and server-side profile without deleting the local wardrobe. Pseudonymous usage counters may be retained for up to 13 months to prevent quota-reset abuse. AI consent can be withdrawn from the same screen. Other privacy requests can be submitted through the contact channel above.

9. Security

Production API connections use HTTPS. Provider secrets are not included in the mobile application. AI endpoints are protected with time-limited server-signed sessions, request rate limits, and persistent daily and monthly usage quotas.
